# Brand guidelines

Product name: **ROOOMTECH Decision Core**

Repository name: **ROOOMTECH-Decision-Core**

Recommended short form: **RTDC** after the full product name has appeared once.

Do not use third-party product names in the product title, repository title, logo, domain name, package name or marketing headline.

Do not market this software using phrases such as "clone", "2.0", "successor", "upgraded edition", "official alternative", "compatible implementation" or wording that could imply sponsorship or affiliation with another vendor.

Do not copy third-party logos, screenshots, visual identity, benchmarks or marketing copy.
